# Selecting and highlighting Bible text

1. Press and hold a word to select it. A handle appears at each end.
2. Drag either handle to expand or shrink the selection, including down to a single word. You can release and drag again without resetting or opening Range Actions.
3. Tap or click inside the selected text to open Range Actions.
4. Tap or click outside the selected text and its handles to deselect it.
5. Tap **Paint** (the paintbrush button) to apply the current highlight color. The color sample labeled **Color Setup** opens setup and does not apply highlighting. Choose a default group with its check-circle button, or create a group by choosing a color, entering a name, and tapping Add. When text is selected, **Apply Highlight** in setup returns to the reader and paints it. Going back without applying keeps the selection. The **?** button in Color Setup explains these steps. Highlight is also available in Range Actions.
6. Clear Markup removes the markup, closes the action sheet, and clears the selection.

These instructions apply to the shared Bible reader on Mac, iPhone, iPad, Android, and Windows. The separate eLibrary reader has its own Help. In the Bible reader menu, the question mark beside Apply Markup opens these instructions.
