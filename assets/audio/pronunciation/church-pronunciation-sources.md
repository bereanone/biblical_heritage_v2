# Shared church-name additions

These speech-only entries supplement the bundled US English Kokoro lexicon. Scripture and book text are unchanged.

- Pergamos: first-syllable stress and unstressed final syllables reflect the user-requested “Perg-ah-mus.” This is a preference, not a claim about reconstructed Greek.
- Thyatira: English reading based on https://www.biblegateway.com/resources/encyclopedia-of-the-bible/Thyatira (accessed 2026-09-20).
- Laodicea: English reading based on https://www.biblegateway.com/resources/encyclopedia-of-the-bible/Laodicea-Laodiceans (accessed 2026-09-20).

Other four church names already have bundled entries. Sound-token checks and generated Mac audio pass; listening approval on each app is separate.
